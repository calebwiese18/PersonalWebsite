ScamShield

A Raspberry Pi device that listens to phone calls on speakerphone, flags scams aimed at older adults in real time, and warns both the caller and their family. Built by a team of three in 24 hours, competing against about 750 students.

What I built

  • Engineered the alert notification pipeline end to end: once a call is flagged, it triggers a spoken warning, local LED and buzzer feedback, and a message to trusted family members.
  • Built the secure family dashboard where relatives see device status, alerts, call transcripts, and timestamps.
  • Integrated ElevenLabs voice synthesis to generate spoken warnings tailored to the call.

How it works

  • Speech is transcribed on the Pi and scored 0–100 for scam risk by Google Gemini, with a keyword check as a fallback if the AI is slow.
  • Score thresholds and cooldowns keep false alarms down, and a physical button lets the user raise an alert manually.
